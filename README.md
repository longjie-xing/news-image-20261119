# The World in One Picture — 19 November 2026

One image that sums up 19 November 2026 world news. Entry for the
make.fun `news-image-20261119` bounty.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the poster itself (1024×1024): six stories from 19 November 2026.
- `main.ts` — copies `entry.png` byte-for-byte to the output path.
  Deterministic: no network, no re-encoding, two runs produce identical bytes.
- `LICENSE` — MIT.

## The six stories

1. Trump, Xi and Putin hold their first trilateral meeting, on the APEC sidelines in Shenzhen.
2. The International Court of Justice rules on the Myanmar / Rohingya genocide case.
3. GTA VI releases, ending a 13-year wait.
4. Anthropic's IPO raises $107.3B.
5. Israel launches a ground operation in central Gaza.
6. Three oil tankers catch fire near the Strait of Hormuz; violence in Bangladesh and COP31.

Sources checked against 19 November 2026 reporting (Japan Times, NDTV, Horizon, Open Chronicle, AP).
